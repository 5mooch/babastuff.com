CapFlow

Move YouTube captions anywhere on the screen

CapFlow takes a YouTube video's captions off the bottom of the screen and shows them as a floating liquid-glass line you can drag anywhere on the video. It's built for deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers, and anyone who wants captions that are easier to follow.

  • Move, resize and restyle the caption line.
  • Captions are read and shown on your device only.
  • Your first hour is free. After that, Lifetime Access is a one-time payment of $24.99.
