CapFlow

Glass captions for YouTube

CapFlow is a Chrome extension that shows a YouTube video's captions in a floating liquid-glass window you can drag anywhere on the video. It's built for deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers, and anyone who wants captions that are easier to follow.

  • Move, resize and restyle the caption window.
  • Captions are read and shown on your device only.
  • Your first hour is free. After that, Lifetime Access is a one-time payment of $24.99.
