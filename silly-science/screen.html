The graveyard

Screen "facts" that get shared constantly but didn't survive a second look.

Paper titles link to the paper's DOI where the pipeline matched it in Crossref and OpenAlex, otherwise to a Google Scholar search. “Free copy” links to an open-access version found by OpenAlex. “Replication record” links to the FORRT replication atlas. Numbers are summarized from the papers and press coverage of them; check the original before quoting one.
Nothing here is medical advice. The Tetris-after-trauma studies in particular were run with researchers; they are not a DIY therapy.
